CoastFI

"When can I stop working?" is one compound-interest question - and there's a quieter milestone before it: the coast point, where your existing savings grow to cover retirement even if you never invest another dollar. Most people pass it without noticing.

Run my numbers

Enter your spending, savings, contributions and expected return. CoastFI gives you three finish lines: your FI number (the 4% rule), how many years until you hit it and at what age, and your coast number - with a badge the day your money can finish the race without you. Everything stays in your browser.
